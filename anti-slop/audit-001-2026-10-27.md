## Numbered Findings List

### Tier 1: Hard Gate (Priority: HIGH)

1. **[Finding #01] R-02 — Copywriting: Em Dash (`—`) in HTML metadata and headings**
   - **Location:** `index.html` (lines 7, 16, 27, 71)
   - **Reason:** Karakter em dash (`—`) dilarang di seluruh user-facing copy; ganti dengan colon (`:`), pipe (`|`), atau koma.
   - **Current:** `<title>Louis Vigo — Visual Specialist & Creative Director</title>`
   - **Proposed Fix:** `<title>Louis Vigo: Visual Specialist & Creative Director</title>` atau `Louis Vigo | Visual Specialist & Creative Director`.

2. **[Finding #02] R-02 — Copywriting: Em Dash (`—`) in profile data and project challenges**
   - **Location:** `src/data/portfolioData.js` (lines 13, 75, 77, 79, 188, 358, 420)
   - **Reason:** Muncul em dash berulang di tagline, subtagline, location, dan narasi bio/challenge.
   - **Current:**
     - Line 13 & 75: `Branding — Social Media — Print Media`
     - Line 77: `Indonesia (UTC+7) — Open Worldwide`
     - Line 79: `I don't just make things look good — I craft strategic visuals...`
     - Line 188: `concepts—curated with experts—into engaging assets`
   - **Proposed Fix:** Ganti separator dengan bullet dot (`•`), koma (`,`), atau tanda kurung `(curated with experts)`.

3. **[Finding #03] R-02 — Copywriting: Em Dash (`—`) in UI components and modals**
   - **Location:** `src/components/Footer.jsx` (line 15), `src/components/CVModal.jsx` (line 100), `src/components/Navbar.jsx` (line 101)
   - **Reason:** Em dash digunakan sebagai pemisah nama di footer dan modal title.
   - **Current:** `(Louis Vigo) — All Rights Reserved.`, `Curriculum Vitae — Louis Vigo`
   - **Proposed Fix:** Ganti dengan dot atau colon: `(Louis Vigo). All Rights Reserved.` dan `Curriculum Vitae: Louis Vigo`.

4. **[Finding #04] R-17 & C-5 — Data & Numbers: Arbitrary percentage competence bars in CV**
   - **Location:** `src/data/portfolioData.js` (lines 115–124)
   - **Reason:** Nilai numerik persentase seperti `Illustrator: 95`, `Photoshop: 95`, `Gemini AI: 95` adalah pola template resume AI yang tidak objektif dan tidak terverifikasi.
   - **Proposed Fix:** Hapus properti `level` numerik; tampilkan murni nama software dan kategorinya (`Vector & Branding`, `Video Production`, dsb.), atau tandai dengan tingkat kemahiran berbasis bukti riil (misal: `Primary Toolkit`, `Production Standard`).

5. **[Finding #05] R-17 & R-36 — Data & Numbers: Unverifiable vanity stat in marquee ticker**
   - **Location:** `src/components/MarqueeTicker.jsx` (lines 8, 19)
   - **Reason:** Teks `"22K+ Viral Engagement"` muncul tanpa konteks proyek atau sumber terverifikasi di marquee banner, menyerupai klaim generic marketing stat.
   - **Proposed Fix:** Ganti dengan spesialisasi nyata atau pencapaian spesifik yang terverifikasi (misal: `"Nationwide Campaigns"`, `"4 Major Cities Coverage"`, atau fokus disiplin).

6. **[Finding #06] R-25 — Color Contrast: Low contrast text on small UI labels**
   - **Location:** `src/components/Footer.jsx` (line 16: `text-[11px] text-white/50` on dark umber), `src/components/CVSection.jsx` (line 124: `text-[10px] text-white/60`), `src/components/MobileFloatingDock.jsx` (inactive lang button)
   - **Reason:** Teks berukuran 10px-11px dengan opasitas 50%-60% menghasilkan rasio kontras < 4.5:1, gagal memenuhi standar WCAG AA untuk readability.
   - **Proposed Fix:** Tingkatkan opasitas menjadi minimal `text-white/80` atau `text-white/90` dan naikkan font size minimal 12px (`text-xs`).

### Tier 2: Purpose-Gate (Priority: MEDIUM)

7. **[Finding #07] R-16 & antislop-copywriting — Negative Parallelism in Bio**
   - **Location:** `src/data/portfolioData.js` (line 79)
   - **Reason:** Konstruksi kalimat *"I don't just make things look good — I craft strategic visuals that communicate clearly and drive real results"* adalah formula AI textbook negative parallelism ("It's not just X, it's Y").
   - **Proposed Fix:** Tulis langsung tanpa formula kontras buatan: *"I craft strategic visuals that communicate clearly and drive measurable business results, translating complex corporate goals into production-grade design."*

8. **[Finding #08] R-16 & antislop-copywriting — Empty AI vocabulary in Footer and Bio**
   - **Location:** `src/components/Footer.jsx` (line 16), `src/data/portfolioData.js` (line 79)
   - **Reason:** Frasa *"Designed with Visual Mastery & Built for High-Impact Communication"* dan *"high-impact visual solutions"* menggunakan buzzword generator abstrak tanpa informasi spesifik.
   - **Proposed Fix:** Ganti dengan pernyataan direct dan grounded: *"Visual communication design, branding systems, and art direction by Louis Vigo."*

9. **[Finding #09] R-04 — Decorative AI Sparkles Icon**
   - **Location:** `src/components/AboutSection.jsx` (line 81: `✨`), `src/components/ContactSection.jsx` (line 43: `<Sparkles />` di badge)
   - **Reason:** Sparkles/bintang adalah ikon generic default AI yang sering ditempelkan tanpa fungsi komunikasi nyata.
   - **Proposed Fix:** Hilangkan emoji `✨` dan ganti `<Sparkles />` di badge ketersediaan dengan indikator status fungsional (misal: active availability green pulse dot atau tanpa dekorasi).

10. **[Finding #10] R-13 & Part 1 — Custom Cursor hijacking**
    - **Location:** `src/components/CustomCursor.jsx`, `src/App.jsx` (line 114)
    - **Reason:** Trailing cursor spring halo ring (`CustomCursor`) dengan ambient glow sering memberi impresi template tech-demo dan dapat mengganggu aksesibilitas/responsivitas kursor native OS di monitor refresh-rate tinggi.
    - **Proposed Fix:** Pertimbangkan untuk menonaktifkan atau memberikan opsi disable kursor kustom, mengembalikan kursor native yang tajam dan responsif.

11. **[Finding #11] R-13 — Ambient radial blur-3xl glow orbs stacking**
    - **Location:** `src/components/CoverSection.jsx` (line 60), `src/components/TiltMockup.jsx` (line 135)
    - **Reason:** Menggunakan `bg-gradient-to-tr from-amber-400/25 via-brand-orange/30 to-amber-200/20 blur-3xl animate-pulse-glow` di belakang setiap mockup dan cover berpotensi menjadi visual fatigue/glow-stacking.
    - **Proposed Fix:** Sederhanakan efek drop-shadow mockup secara natural tanpa orb gradient glow besar di background.
